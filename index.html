Research

Operational Human Oversight for autonomous and agentic systems

My current work focuses on translating high-level Responsible AI principles into operational governance mechanisms. The central question is not merely whether humans should remain “in the loop,” but where human authority should be triggered, how escalation should work, who is qualified to intervene, what actions are permitted, and what evidence should be preserved.
