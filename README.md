# Lei Gao

## AI Governance & Human Oversight Researcher

**Research focus:**  
Operational Human Oversight · Agentic AI Governance · Responsible AI · Trustworthy AI · International AI Standardization

I am a researcher working on how meaningful human intervention can be operationally embedded into increasingly autonomous and agentic AI systems.

My current work focuses on intervention triggers, escalation, qualified human review, override authority, temporary suspension, resumption, auditability, traceability, and accountability across AI trust lifecycles.

---

## Current Research

### ITU-T FG-TIDA — Theme #16

**Operational Human Oversight Integration for Agentic AI Trust Systems**

Proposer of Theme #16 within the public working process of ITU-T FG-TIDA.

The theme examines operational Human Oversight in agentic AI trust systems, including:

- intervention triggers
- escalation criteria
- qualified human review
- human override
- temporary hold and suspension
- resumption and rollback
- auditability and traceability
- accountability and evidence requirements

Public working page:  
https://github.com/FG-TIDA/themes/issues/16

---

### ITU-T FG-AI4SSC — FGAI4SSC-I-015

**Human Oversight in AI-Enabled Smart Sustainable Cities**

Author of formal input document **FGAI4SSC-I-015** to the ITU-T Focus Group on AI for Smart Sustainable Cities and Communities.

The contribution explores operational Human Oversight mechanisms in AI-enabled urban systems, including intervention triggers, escalation, qualified human review, override authority, traceable logging, and accountable decision processes.
