## Research focus

Operational Human Oversight · Agentic AI Governance · Responsible AI · Smart Sustainable Cities · System Dynamics · Digital Governance

I study how meaningful human authority can remain operationally embedded in increasingly autonomous AI systems — through intervention, escalation, qualified review, override, suspension, resumption, auditability and accountability.
