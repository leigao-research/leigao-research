      03 / Working research instrument

      Change the conditions. Inspect the oversight decision.

      This transparent rule-based instrument specifies action boundaries, responsible roles and evidence needed for a decision. Results update immediately when inputs change; a recorded approval is always tied to the current version of the proposed action.

      Hypothetical workflow configuration

      Current Lexa is restricted to its documented information role. Other sector settings use illustrative generic rules; actual authority structures require independent validation.

      This instrument evaluates proposed organisational rules, not real Lexa outputs. Availability of an approver is not evidence of approval; a material change invalidates any prior approval in this demonstration.

      05 / Public-interface research protocol

      Keep observations separate from hypotheses.

      The original marriage-registration exchange is the only public response documented in this project material. The prompts below are proposed further observations; they have not been executed or independently verified here.

      Research practice

      Use the official site manually, ask only public-service questions, preserve the exact Ukrainian answer, date and cited sources. Do not submit personal data or conduct high-volume automated testing.

      Visit the official Lexa ↗

      No form entries are sent to a server. Browser storage is optional and may be unavailable; the export includes your unsaved current form. Do not record personal data.

      06 / Research pathway

      A research method that can travel beyond Lexa.

      The contribution is not a claim to own the live assistant. It is a method for connecting AI-assisted decisions to an authorised human role, an intervention trigger and a verifiable evidence trail. Research hosts can adapt and independently evaluate this design.

      Public administration

      Information governance

      Trace a change in an official service requirement to a reviewed, versioned and checked public answer.

      Organisations and business

      Delegated decision authority

      Study approval thresholds, fallback authority and evidence for AI-prepared external actions.

      Human–AI research

      Intervention and resumption

      Specify when a task is held, what must change, and which evidence authorises its resumption.

      What can be studied next?

      Scenario-based expert review, inter-rater agreement on intervention triggers, false-release versus false-hold trade-offs, and the completeness of human authorisation evidence. These are proposed studies, not completed experiments.

      Research boundary

      The decision laboratory tests consistency with proposed rules. It does not measure deployed Lexa effectiveness, establish legal compliance, confirm Ministry procedures or supply independently validated outcomes.
