02 / From observation to control design

The difference is not a better chatbot answer. It is an accountable decision process.

Consider a hypothetical change in marriage-registration requirements. We can compare what the public exchange tells us with the additional governance steps a research framework would propose. This isnota before-and-after test of changes made to Lexa.

What the public exchange shows

A useful service answer

Lexa presents required documents, application routes and legal references. The exchange alone does not reveal how changes in the authoritative rules propagate to the public-facing answer.

Unanswered by this observation
  • Who detects a relevant rule change?
  • Who approves revised service content?
  • How is the new answer checked?
  • How is a disputed answer routed for review?
Proposed research contribution

An auditable oversight workflow

Specify an accountable content owner, review trigger, source/version check, authorised publication decision and post-update answer verification.

Proposed evidence chain
  • Authoritative source and effective date
  • Content-owner review and approval
  • Versioned knowledge-base change
  • Representative public-answer check
  • Escalation or closure record
Research integrity:The left-hand side describes what can and cannot be inferred from a public response. The right-hand side is a proposed framework. Neither side establishes what Lexa's current internal oversight process is, nor whether the researcher's suggestions have been implemented.
03 / Working research instrument

Change the conditions. Watch the oversight decision change.

This is a transparent, rule-based prototype for analysing organisational control points, not a second AI model and not a component installed in Lexa. It avoids treating “always ask a human” as the universal answer.

Hypothetical workflow configuration

A governance hypothesis is not an institutional policy. Actor roles, legal powers, escalation thresholds and retention periods require host-specific validation.

05 / For prospective research hosts

A transferable method, not a claim of product ownership.

The research contribution is to specify who may decide, when human intervention is necessary, what evidence must be retained and how a paused operation can lawfully resume. The public-sector case makes these design questions concrete; the same questions can be investigated in other organisational settings.

面向国内企业或合作机构：可以先选一个低风险、非敏感、可授权访问的业务流程，梳理“业务责任人—审批节点—异常升级—证据留存—恢复授权”，再由机构内部负责人核实。这是一套研究与试点评估方法，不代表具备任何行业资质认证或监管批准。
