FG-TIDA

Theme #16 — Operational Human Oversight Integration for Agentic AI Trust Systems

Public working proposal focused on intervention triggers, escalation, qualified human review, human override, suspension, evidence requirements and return-to-operation conditions in agentic AI trust systems.

Public working page identifies Lei Gao as proposer. This is an open working theme, not an adopted ITU Recommendation. Theme #16 ↗

October 2026 update

Theme #16 — current working position

Public working proposalProposer: Lei GaoStatus checked: 26 October 2026
  • Working structure: Consolidated Working Structure v1; requirements discussion baseline: Matrix v0.2.
  • UC-6 Stage 0: bounded, reproducible calibration of selected mapping semantics. It does not demonstrate meaningful or authenticated human review, operational oversight capacity, live enforcement or successful control transfer.
  • UC-4: deferred-action mapping remains a candidate, dependent on an originating versioned source case and agreed expected observations.
  • Candidate D1 Requirements v0.1: shared for technical review; not an adopted requirement set.
  • Operator-facing assessment: candidate protocol design only; not experimental-ready and not evidence of validated oversight effectiveness.

Preparatory working materials only; not an adopted ITU-T specification, Working Group charter, formal deliverable or institutional decision.

Emerging research agenda

Human Oversight under Institutional Disruption and Crisis

An emerging line of inquiry examines how legitimate human authority, intervention and accountability can be preserved or restored when institutions, personnel, delegated mandates, evidence channels or essential public-service arrangements are disrupted.
