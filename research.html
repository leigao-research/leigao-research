30 October 2026 working update

Theme #16 — current working position

Public working proposalProposer: Lei GaoStatus checked: 30 October 2026
  • Working baseline: Consolidated Working Structure v1; Requirements Matrix v0.2 and the published UC-6 calibration remain unchanged.
  • UC-6 Stage 0: a bounded, reproducible calibration of selected mapping semantics only. It does not establish meaningful or authenticated human review, operational oversight capacity, live enforcement or successful control transfer.
  • UC-4: deferred-action mapping remains a candidate and depends on an agreed versioned source case and expected observations.
  • Candidate D1 Requirements v0.1: shared for technical review; it does not replace Matrix v0.2 and is not an adopted requirement set.
  • Operator-facing assessment: the current term is “operator-facing experimental assessment package”, rather than “validation package”. Its prerequisites — the versioned source case, expected observations, protocol scope, independence arrangements, fallback conditions and closure criteria — remain to be agreed. It is not experiment-ready and is not evidence of validated oversight effectiveness.

The discussion keeps evidence availability and presentation, human notice and understanding, technical execution and acknowledgement, and independently observed effects distinct. A fallback should specify its trigger, responsible authority and expected effect.

As proposer, Lei Gao has contributed to consolidating the working structure, preparing Candidate D1 requirements for technical review, and helping coordinate discussion of use-case prerequisites and the separately scoped operator-facing assessment. This is a contribution within an open working process, not a formally appointed Working Group leadership role.

These are preparatory working materials, not an adopted ITU-T specification, Working Group charter, formal deliverable or institutional decision.

Selected research questions

Questions guiding the current research programme

These are developing research questions, not reported empirical findings.

Persuasive but incorrect AI adviceWhen AI advice is persuasive but wrong, under what conditions can actionable human oversight reduce over-reliance and improve decision quality?
Unavailable or ineffective supervisionWhat should an agentic AI system do when legitimate human supervision becomes unavailable, unreliable or too slow to intervene?
Revalidation before resumptionHow should delegated authority, evidence and system state be revalidated before AI-supported operations resume?
Restoring legitimate controlHow can legitimate human decision authority and accountability be restored when institutions, personnel or evidence channels are disrupted?

Emerging research agenda

Human Oversight under Institutional Disruption and Crisis

This line extends the core Operational Human Oversight programme into high-disruption settings, including prolonged emergencies and the recovery of essential public services. The central question remains how legitimate human control can be preserved, constrained and restored when ordinary oversight conditions fail.
